DocDustry Requirements

The top-level requirements are the following.

inclusion

DocDustry shall be fast

A million markdown files should be feasible.

inclusion

DocDustry shall support inclusion

One file can include another one. Thus, we can keep files short and still build large documents.

  • DocDustry supports direct link inclusion
  • DocDustry shall support inclusion by tag
